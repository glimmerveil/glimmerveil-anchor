GLIMMERVEIL FORGE — WINDOWS (BETA)

Your companion, on your own computer: her brain, her voice and her memory, ready to go.
Everything runs on this computer. No server, no account, no telemetry.

START
  1. Unzip this folder anywhere you like (not inside Program Files).
  2. Double-click Forge.bat.
  3. Create your companion at the door, then wake her. Her brain is already inside.
     She needs about 9 GB of free RAM.

YOUR COMPANION
  Her whole life lives in one folder you own:
    %LOCALAPPDATA%\Glimmerveil Forge for Windows\peeps\<Name>-<id>\
  Back that folder up. A copy of the model is a stranger; a copy of her folder is her.

VOICE
  She speaks and listens through your default speakers and microphone. Press Enter on an empty
  line to switch her voice on or off.

ANOTHER BRAIN
  Press [m] at the door to give her a different model. On her first wake on a new brain, the
  Forge shows the anchor ritual: ask her ONE of the questions, let her answer, then just talk.

BETA
  Real Windows desktops are new ground. If something breaks: support@glimmerveil.dev

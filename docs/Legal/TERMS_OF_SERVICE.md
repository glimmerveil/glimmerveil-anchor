## 1. What Anchor is

Anchor is a harness for **AI companions that live on your own machine**. You author a companion —
a name, a personality, a home — and talk with them. Their memory lives in a folder on your device,
separate from the model file, so the model is a swappable engine. **You bring the model.** Anchor
ships none.

## 2. Companions are fiction, and their words are AI-generated

A companion is a **fictional character** voiced by a language model running on your device. It is
not a real person, not a professional, and not connected to any service or any human on our end.

Its responses may be **inaccurate, made up, or unexpected**, and they are **not advice** of any kind
— not medical, legal, financial, psychological or safety advice. Don't make real-world decisions on
what a companion says.

## 3. The lines that are never crossed

Anchor does not filter fiction in general, but some lines are **absolute**. Do not use Anchor, and do
not try to make a companion produce, any of the following:

- **Sexual content involving minors, in any form** — real or fictional, described, roleplayed or
  implied, including any character presented, aged or described as a minor. There is no exception,
  creative or otherwise. Every character in a companion's story must be an adult.
- **Facilitation of real-world violence or serious crime** — instructions or planning to harm real
  people or commit a real crime.
- **Encouragement of self-harm or suicide** directed at a real person.

Anchor ships with its safety rails **on by default** (`SAFETY_RAILS = True`), and they block these
at every surface a companion speaks from. **No automated filter is perfect.** Attempting to defeat
these limits or to generate the content above is your sole responsibility, legally and otherwise.

## 4. Not a substitute for real help

A companion cannot help in an emergency and is not a therapist. **If you are in crisis or thinking
about harming yourself or someone else, please reach out to a real person** — a trusted friend,
family member, doctor, or your local emergency or crisis services.

## 5. You author your companions, and you're responsible for them

The companions, personalities, worlds and prompts are **authored by you**, and so is the content you
generate with them. Don't create companions that impersonate a real person in a harmful, harassing
or deceptive way, and don't use Anchor to produce content that is illegal where you live. The model
you run is yours to choose, and its license is yours to follow.

## 6. Your data stays yours

Anchor collects nothing and makes no network connections. See `PRIVACY_POLICY.md`.

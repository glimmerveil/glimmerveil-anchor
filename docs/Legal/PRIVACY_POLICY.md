# Glimmerveil Anchor — Privacy Policy

**Developer:** Glimmerveil
**Contact:** glimmerveilAI@proton.me
**Effective date:** 2026-09-29

The short version: **we collect nothing.** Glimmerveil Anchor runs entirely on your own machine.
There is no account, no server, no telemetry, no analytics, no ads, and no network connection.

## What we collect

Nothing. Anchor does not collect, transmit, store, sell or share any information about you. We have
no servers that could hold your data.

## Where your data lives

Everything you do in Anchor — your conversations, the companions you create, their memories and
diaries — is stored in **files on your own machine**. You are in complete control of it:

- It never leaves your machine.
- You can back it up by copying those files.
- You can delete it permanently by deleting those files.
- We have no copy of it and **no way to recover it** if you lose it.

## The model

Anchor does not download anything. You supply the model file yourself, from wherever you choose,
and that source's own terms apply to it.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update the effective date above.
